# HodgeCY II Title Candidates

## Recommendation

Primary title:

> HodgeCY II: Source Fidelity and Structural Block-Evaluation Collapse for
> Double-Octic Calabi--Yau Threefolds

This is the best fit because it names the actual contribution: source fidelity,
the verified block-evaluation collapse, and the double-octic setting. It avoids
overclaiming classification, classical nodal defects, or Hodge-atom
identifications.

## Required Candidate Ranking

1. E. HodgeCY II: Source Fidelity and Structural Block-Evaluation Collapse for
   Double-Octic Calabi--Yau Threefolds
2. C. Source Fidelity Beyond Hodge Data: Integral Separation and Structural
   Collapse for Double-Octic Calabi--Yau Threefolds
3. A. HodgeCY II: Integral Source Separation and Structural Evaluation Collapse
   in Double-Octic Calabi--Yau Threefolds
4. B. Integral Source Fidelity and Hilbert--Burch Collapse in Double-Octic
   Calabi--Yau Threefolds
5. D. HodgeCY II: Source Fidelity Beyond Hodge Data for Double-Octic
   Calabi--Yau Threefolds

Alternate title:

> Source Fidelity Beyond Hodge Data: Integral Separation and Structural
> Collapse for Double-Octic Calabi--Yau Threefolds

Candidate E is preferred for continuity with HodgeCY I and for naming the
block-evaluation layer explicitly.  Candidate C is the best alternate because
it is readable and emphasizes the progression beyond Hodge data.  Candidate A
is accurate but slightly heavier.  Candidate B foregrounds Hilbert--Burch well
but can sound too narrow.  Candidate D is clear but underspecifies the structural
collapse, which is the final HodgeCY II theorem-bearing contribution.

## Avoid

- Titles centered on "classification" unless the subtitle makes clear that the
  456-presentation census is source-level.
- Titles claiming "node defect" as a theorem.
- Titles claiming "Hodge atom" as the proved mechanism.
- Titles implying all 114 sets have been geometrically analyzed.
