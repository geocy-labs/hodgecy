# Related Work Notes

## Conservative Literature Position

HodgeCY II should be positioned as a source-fidelity and structural
block-evaluation paper.  It should not be framed as the first observation of
large Calabi--Yau collisions: large degeneracies under Hodge numbers and other
coarse invariants are already well documented.

## Context Points

- Kreuzer and Skarke provide the large-scale toric hypersurface backdrop:
  473,800,776 reflexive four-polytopes collapse to 30,108 Hodge-number pairs.
- Ashmore and He document large statistical degeneracies in Calabi--Yau
  threefold data, including very large same-Hodge fibers.  This rejects claims
  like "no previous work found more than 10,000 collisions" when collision
  means agreement under Hodge numbers or similarly coarse data.
- Cynk and van Straten are the closest double-octic comparison point.  Their
  refinement uses Picard--Fuchs/period data; HodgeCY II instead uses
  source-assembly, rational/integral lattice, equivariant, and verified
  block-evaluation layers.
- Grimm, Ruehle, and van de Heisteeg give a degeneration-sensitive
  classification context via limiting mixed Hodge structures.  HodgeCY II does
  not identify its source assemblies or Hodge atoms with LMHS invariants.
- Brodie, Constantin, Lukas, and Ruehle provide adjacent precedent for
  systematic comparison across construction data.
- Chandra, Constantin, Fraser-Taliente, Harvey, and Lukas refine
  Kreuzer--Skarke data using topological information such as intersection data,
  Chern classes, and integral equivalence checks.  HodgeCY II's refinement
  ladder is different, not a replacement.
- Geramita, Harbourne, and Migliore give the star-configuration and
  basic-double-link background.  Because full proper-meeting hypotheses are
  stronger than the verified \(84/84a\) hypotheses, the manuscript should use
  the explicit Hilbert--Burch proof for the ideal identity and cite their work
  as structural context.
- Burch, Bruns--Herzog, and Eisenbud provide standard Hilbert--Burch
  references for the codimension-two resolution.

## Novelty Statement To Use

Large degeneracies of Calabi--Yau data under Hodge numbers and other coarse
invariants are known.  HodgeCY II contributes a source-aware computational
fidelity census and a verified witness where source-level integral separation
coexists with structural block-evaluation collapse.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not say HodgeCY II proves all 456 presentations are geometrically
  classified.
- Do not say the block schemes are ordinary-node schemes.
- Do not say the block-evaluation deficiency is the classical nodal defect.
- Do not say the source assembly is an LMHS or Hodge-atom invariant.
- Do not say the paper resolves the source-to-node comparison problem.
