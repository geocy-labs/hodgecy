# HodgeCY II Manuscript Plan

## Recommended Position

HodgeCY II should be written as a fidelity and structural-explanation paper,
not as a complete classification paper. The paper's central contribution is a
verified non-monotone comparison:

1. a complete source-level census of the frozen 456-presentation double-octic
   universe;
2. a source-fidelity witness pair, `84/84a`, with equal local data, equal Hodge
   data, and equal rational source assembly, but distinct integral Smith type;
3. a verified block-geometry/evaluation layer where the same witness pair
   collapses again;
4. a self-contained Hilbert--Burch explanation of that block-evaluation
   collapse under the stated eight-plane hypotheses.

## Scope Boundary

The manuscript should not claim:

- a complete geometric analysis of all 114 nontrivial source-level sets;
- ordinary-node promotion for `84/84a`;
- equality between the verified block schemes and full saturated Jacobian node
  schemes;
- a classical nodal defect theorem;
- a natural source-to-evaluation or source-to-node chain map;
- an integral evaluation-lattice theorem;
- an LMHS/MHM/Hodge-atom identification.

These nonclaims should appear early in the introduction and again near the
main theorem/evidence matrix.
